Privacy Policy

Curtina Schools mobile app · Last updated: 13 October 2026

The Curtina Schools app is provided by {{COMPANY NAME}} ("we", "us"). It gives students, parents and staff access to their school's online portal, which runs on our school ERP software. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Who is responsible for your data

Your school decides what information it keeps about students, parents and staff, and is responsible for it. We host and operate the school portal on the school's behalf and only process this information to provide the service to the school.

Information the app handles

  • Your selected school. Stored only on your device so the app opens your school's portal next time.
  • Login details and portal content. When you sign in, your admission number or employee code and password are sent securely (HTTPS) to your school's portal. Information you see or enter in the portal, such as attendance, fees, results, homework and messages, is stored by the school's ERP system.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, the app gets a device token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and sends it to your school's portal so the school can send you notifications such as announcements, homework and fee reminders.
  • Photos and files. Only photos or files that you choose to upload, for example a profile photo or an assignment, are sent to the school's portal.
  • Camera, microphone and location. Used only when a portal page asks for them and you give permission, for example to take a photo to upload or to mark attendance on campus. You can turn these permissions off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • The app has no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't use your information for any purpose other than providing the school portal to your school.

Service providers

Notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service. These services receive only the device token and the notification content.

Data retention and deletion

Your school keeps portal records according to its own policies and legal obligations. To access, correct or delete your information, or to close your account, please contact your school, or contact us at the address below and we will work with your school to handle your request. You can remove the app's data from your device at any time by deleting the app.

Children

Student accounts are created and managed by the school. Parents or guardians who have questions about their child's information should contact the school or us.

Security

All communication between the app and the school portal is encrypted using HTTPS.
